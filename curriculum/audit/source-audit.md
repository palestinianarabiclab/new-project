## Entry requirements stated by the book

The learner is expected to know the Arabic letters and vowel marks and to be
able to read at an elementary level. The book is a study guide to be used with
listening, recitation, and correction from a qualified Qur'an teacher.

## Coverage boundaries declared by the source

The book states that Part Two will cover:

- characteristics of letters
- tafkheem and tarqeeq
- relationships between letters and their categories
- rules for hamzat al-wasl

The book states that Part Three will cover:

- stopping and starting
- words cut off and words joined in writing
- feminine written ha/ta issues
- words specific to the recitation of Hafs

These subjects must not be presented as Part One lessons.

## Source and verification cautions

- Qur'anic phrases are printed as scanned images. Every phrase must be manually
  transcribed and checked against the page image before publication.
- Audio, timing, and articulation performance require review by a qualified
  Tajweed teacher. Text verification alone cannot certify recitation quality.
- The distinction between PDF page and printed page is retained even though
  they align in this file.
- The source uses English transliterations inconsistently by modern editorial
  standards. A project-wide transliteration policy must be selected without
  changing the author's Arabic terminology.
